# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-14)

- **matchcut:** the front door. Three films (product reel, matchcut film, story short), the beats, the run, delivery formats and the letterbox spec.
- **keyframes:** image prompting for film stills: seven style blocks (photoreal space, product hero, live action and more), a master prompt that ends on the match frame, composition rules, three-frame moves; notes for GPT Image, Midjourney, Flux, Gemini and Seedream.
- **clip-prompts:** the story-carrying prompt block, frame modes, lengths and a fixes table; guides for MiniMax, Grok, Seedance, Veo, Kling, Wan, Runway, Luma and LTX; a three-clip worked example.
- **ui-reel:** the slow-motion recorder (rAF on the slowed clock, navigation-safe, typing, phone takes, rehearsal runs), storyboards, the 60 fps encoder, and a Remotion template with a title intro made in code (dots that land as the wordmark, a cut line that splits it, then swirl into the product's first shape), an optional cold-open section, the virtual camera, captions, cut blooms, a phone frame, an end card with a mark and wordmark, and `check-tl.mjs` (run by `npm run check`), which fails a cut that lands on a camera still travelling to its subject.
- **score:** two ways to the music: the code band (an original soundtrack from score.json, nothing to sign up for), or a produced song from any music generator, with `song_prompt.py` writing the prompt from the film's timeline and mood (presets, one shape per tool) and `song_fit.py` measuring the tempo, bars and hits and lining the cuts up with the beats; `score.py` mixes either (`track`, `track_curve`, `fx_gain`) and falls back to the code band. Also `cues.py` placing a sound on every click, keystroke, cut, caption and the end card plus the story sounds a timeline lists (`sfx`), with a bundled effects kit (public-domain recordings and sounds made in code), `make_kit.py` to make a whole kit in code and `sfx_prompts.py` to prompt one from any sound-effect generator, the mix, and a voiceover mixer that works with a limited ffmpeg.
- **examples/demo:** Orbit, a mission-control app with a live Earth, a launch and a first-light image downlink, and the 36 second matchcut film made from it, opening on its own title intro. `scripts/doctor.mjs` checks a machine.
